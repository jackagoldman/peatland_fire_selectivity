Peatland and Canopy Cover Map for the Canadian Boreal Region
NicholasPontone (February 2024) <NicholasPontone@cmail.carleton.ca>


This map is a combination of two publically available data products, a map of peatland sub-classes from Pontone et al. (2024), and 
a canopy cover map from Guindon et al. (2024).


PPC = Peatland Permafrost Complex
Open = <10% cover
Treed = 10-25% cover
Forested = >25% cover


Citations:

Pontone, N., Millard, K., Thompson, D.K., Guindon, L. and Beaudoin, A. (2024), A hierarchical, multi-sensor framework for peatland sub-class and vegetation mapping throughout the Canadian boreal forest. Remote Sens Ecol Conserv. https://doi.org/10.1002/rse2.384

Guindon, L., Manka, F., Correia, D.L.P., Villemaire, P., Smiley, B., Bernier, P., Gauthier, S., Beaudoin, A., Boucher, J., and Boulanger, Y. 2024. A new approach for Spatializing the Canadian National Forest Inventory (SCANFI) using Landsat dense time series. Can. J. For. Res. https://doi.org/10.1139/cjfr-2023-0118
